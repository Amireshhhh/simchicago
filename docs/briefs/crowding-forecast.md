# Brief: Forecasting next week's crowding per route and station

Generated by `python -m simchi.briefs` from `reports/ops/crowding.json`. Gating test: crowding forecast, bus and 'L' (criteria in the docstring of simchi/ops/crowding.py) — passed.

## Finding

A one-week-ahead ridership forecast (median of the last 4 same-type days) beats "same day last week" for every bus route and 'L' station tested: mean absolute log error 0.134 vs 0.157 on buses (15% lower) and 0.128 vs 0.151 on 'L' stations (15% lower).

## Evidence

- Test period 2026-01-01 to 2026-08-31: 27,946 bus-route forecasts (128 routes) and 34,992 station forecasts (144 stations), each made with data up to the week before.
- Mean absolute error in rides: 413 vs 485 (bus), 262 vs 310 ('L').
- Better on every day type for buses: weekday 0.129 vs 0.151, Saturday 0.142 vs 0.169, Sunday/holiday 0.158 vs 0.184.
- Both periods are after CTA's 2025-01-01 ridership restatement (same counting method).

## What we don't know

- Whether a better daily forecast changes any decision: the test scores forecasts, not outcomes.
- Crowding is riders per scheduled trip per day; within-day peaks and load per vehicle are not forecast (no load data are used).
- Holidays and events are not modelled; the error on unusual days is not reported separately.

## Cost

Not costed: no cost figure for operating the forecast is in reports/. It uses only published daily ridership and GTFS.

## Pilot proposal

Publish the forecast weekly to one CTA planning team for 8 weeks, for the routes and stations they choose, and log each decision it informed.

**Success criterion (fixed before the pilot):** Over the 8 weeks, the forecast's mean absolute log error is below that of "same day last week" for the chosen routes and stations, computed the same way as in the test.
