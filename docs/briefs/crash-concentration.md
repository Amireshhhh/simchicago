# Brief (measured fact): Serious crashes are spread thinly across Chicago's streets

Generated by `python -m simchi.briefs` from `reports/safety/crash_ranking.json`, `reports/safety/robustness.json`. Type: measured fact, not a tested prediction. Checks: W4 measurement (simchi/safety/crashes.py) and the pre-registered robustness checks D (simchi/safety/robustness.py).

**Descriptive, not causal.** This brief reports a measurement and how stable it is. It does not say what causes it or what a change would achieve; no intervention has been tested.

## Measurement

Serious traffic crashes (deaths and incapacitating injuries) in Chicago are spread over many street locations. The 100 street sites with the most serious crashes in 2018-2024 held 4.64% of 2025's serious crashes (71 of 1,530), out of 76,522 street sites. Even the 100 sites that turned out to have the most serious crashes in a year held only 10.98%-13.61% of that year's (2019-2024).

## What we don't know

- Why crashes are spread this way, and whether treating the worst sites would reduce them: nothing here tests an intervention.
- Traffic volume is counted at few sites, so these are counts, not rates per vehicle.
- Vision Zero records begin in 2019; reporting of recent months is still being completed.
